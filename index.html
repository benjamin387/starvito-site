What we do

We buy cars at the end of their COE

Starvito Pte Ltd is a Singapore car buyer dealing in scrap and export vehicles. Tell us the car and we will tell you what it is worth — and whether it is better scrapped or exported.

The whole car, not just the paperwork

We buy the vehicle including its PARF and COE rebate entitlement. You are paid on handover rather than waiting weeks for the rebate to come through from LTA.

Scrap or export, assessed properly

Some cars are worth more exported whole, others are worth more dismantled. We work out which route your car belongs to before quoting, because that decision is what changes the number.

Any condition

Running or not. Accident damage, failed inspection, engine gone, sitting in a carpark for months — all of it still has value, and we will tell you honestly how much.

Paperwork handled

Deregistration and disposal documentation is dealt with as part of the transaction, so you are not working through LTA submissions on your own.

Before you decide

Renew, or let it go?

Everyone in this market has already picked a side of that decision for you. Lenders want you to renew. Dealers want to sell you a new car. Scrapyards want the car. We will run your actual numbers and tell you which one wins — even when the answer is that you should keep it.

Option 01

Renew the COE

Makes sense when the car is mechanically sound and the cost of the PQP is less than what replacing it would cost you over the same period.

Option 02

Scrap or export

Makes sense when the repair bill is approaching what the car is worth, or when the export value on your particular model is unusually strong.
